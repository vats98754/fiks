# Publication options for the fiks paper (researched 2026-10-29)

Paper: "fiks: Turning Silent Tool Failures into Gated Repair Pull Requests for MCP Servers". It is a system design, a small pilot on seeded drifts, and a pre-registration draft for a confirmatory study. Single author (University of Waterloo), with heavy AI assistance disclosed. The current build is 16 pp in `article` class with natbib/plainnat, and the arXiv tarball ships `main.bbl`.

Method: every date and rule below was read on the official page on 2026-10-29, and the URL is given. Anything I could not confirm on an official page is marked **UNVERIFIED**. ## 1. arXiv (as of 2026-10-29)

### 1.1 Endorsement (first-time submitter)
- Help page: https://info.arxiv.org/help/endorsement.html. It says "arXiv requires that users be endorsed before submitting their first paper to arXiv or a new category." It also says "At least one positive endorsement is required per endorsement category".
- **Policy change on 2026-01-21:** https://blog.arxiv.org/2026/01/21/attention-authors-updated-endorsement-policy/. The post says "As of January 21, 2026, arXiv will no longer accept institutional email addresses" as the sole qualifier for endorsing new authors. There are now two routes:
  - **Automatic:** an institutional email **plus** prior authorship of a paper already accepted in the same endorsement domain.
  - **Personal:** an endorsement from "an established arXiv author in the same endorsement domain."
  - arXiv staff cannot waive these requirements.
- **What this means for the paper:** a uwaterloo.ca address alone no longer qualifies. If Anvay has no prior cs paper on arXiv, he needs a personal endorser, such as an advisor or a cs author with endorsement rights. The endorser enters the 6-character code from the endorsement request email. Endorsers "should check that the paper is appropriate for the subject area"; endorsement is not peer review.
- **Domains and cross-lists:** endorsement domains are "high-level subject areas" except in physics. That suggests one cs endorsement covers cs.SE, cs.AI and cs.LG, but the page does not name cs explicitly (**UNVERIFIED**). Neither page says whether cross-lists need endorsement. Moderators "may remove" cross-lists they judge inappropriate (https://info.arxiv.org/help/moderation/index.html).
- **Rules on endorsement in cs.SE, cs.AI and cs.LG specifically:** no category-specific rules were found on the endorsement page.

### 1.2 CS rule on review and position papers (2025-10-31)
- Source: https://blog.arxiv.org/2025/10/31/attention-authors-updated-practice-for-review-articles-and-position-papers-in-arxiv-cs-category/
- The rule: review articles and position papers "must now be accepted at a journal or a conference" and "complete successful peer review" before submission to CS. Authors "must include documentation of successful peer review". Without it, submissions "will be likely to be rejected."
- **Does it apply here?** No. fiks is a research article with a built system and measured pilot results. Keep it framed that way.
- **Related risk:** the moderation page lists "course projects, research proposals, news" among submissions that "may be declined" (https://info.arxiv.org/help/moderation/index.html). The content-types page lists "Proposals for future research" as typically not accepted (https://info.arxiv.org/help/policies/content-types.html).
  - So the paper must not read as a protocol-only proposal. Lead with the system and the pilot, and keep the pre-registration as one section.
  - Do not post the pre-registration alone to arXiv. If it gets Stage 1 acceptance, OSF Preprints is the venue the RR tracks themselves name.

### 1.3 Policy on AI-generated and AI-assisted content
- **Standing policy** ("Policy for authors' use of generative AI language tools"): https://info.arxiv.org/help/moderation/index.html#policy-for-authors-use-of-generative-ai-language-tools
  - Authors must "report in their work any significant use of sophisticated tools", and this "include[s] in particular text-to-text generative AI".
  - Authors "each individually take full responsibility for all its contents, irrespective of how the contents were generated".
  - "generative AI language tools should not be listed as an author".
- **Enforcement from May 2026:** announced by the arXiv CS section chair (T. Dietterich) on X on 2026-05-14. I found no official arXiv page stating it (**UNVERIFIED**). Secondary reports agree:
  - If a submission shows "incontrovertible evidence" that the authors did not check LLM output (hallucinated references, leftover AI meta-comments, placeholder text), the penalty is a one-year ban. After the ban, later submissions must first be accepted at a peer-reviewed venue.
  - Sources: https://www.researchinformation.info/news/arxiv-imposes-one-year-ban-for-unchecked-ai-generated-content/ (2026-05-18) and https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/16/research-repository-arxiv-will-ban-authors-for-a-year-if-they-let-ai-do-all-the-work/
- **How to disclose:**
  - Keep the named "AI use and ethics statement" section (already in `main.tex`).
  - Name each tool and say what it did: code, analysis, text, figures.
  - State that the author checked every reference and number.
  - Keep "AI-assisted (see the AI use statement)" in the Comments field, as `arxiv/metadata.txt` already does.
  - Before upload, run a reference-existence check, because a hallucinated citation is the named trigger for the ban.

### 1.4 Submission limits
- **Rate limit** (from 2026-10-01): https://blog.arxiv.org/2026/10/01/updated-rate-limit-policy/
  - Each submitter may make "up to two submissions per calendar month, with a limit of three total active submissions at any given time".
  - This "applies to all submissions, across all categories".
  - Rejected submissions count toward the limit. Papers deleted before announcement do not.
- **Abstract:** "abstracts longer than 1920 characters will not be accepted" (https://info.arxiv.org/help/prep.html). `arxiv/abstract.txt` is 1917 bytes including the newline. That fits, but there is no margin, so do not add text.
- **Title:** no length limit is stated. Do not use all caps or unicode.
- **File size:** https://info.arxiv.org/help/sizes.html states no numeric limit (it says to contact admins for an exception). Since February 2026, arXiv warns when an image is larger than 34 megapixels.

### 1.5 Source format and .bbl
- Source: https://info.arxiv.org/help/submit_tex.html
- arXiv offers "TeX Live 2023 and 2025 (the default)".
- For non-biblatex documents, "we use `bibtex`". However, "Our system will use the `.bbl` file if it is present", and "the name of the `.bbl` file _must_ match the name of the main `.tex` file".
- For biblatex, the paper and the `.bbl` "must be created by the same program", and TeX Live 2025 accepts only bbl format 3.3.
- The tarball (`main.tex` + `main.bbl` + `generated/*.tex`, natbib/plainnat) meets these rules. Build it once under TeX Live 2025 to confirm.

### 1.6 Licenses
- Source: https://info.arxiv.org/help/license/index.html
- Options: CC BY 4.0; CC BY-SA 4.0; CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; arXiv.org perpetual, non-exclusive license 1.0; CC Zero.
- "The license chosen is irrevocable and cannot be changed", but each new version can carry a different license.
- Recommendation: choose CC BY 4.0, or the arXiv non-exclusive license if a later IEEE or ACM copyright transfer is a concern. Both are compatible with the venues below, which all allow preprints.

## 3. Recommendations

### For the paper as it is now (system + pilot + pre-registration draft)
1. **Registered report, Stage 1: SANER 2027 RR (abstract 30 Oct, report 6 Nov 2026), or MSR 2027 RR (abstract 13 Nov, report 20 Nov 2026) if 30 Oct is too soon.**
   - Cut `prereg/PREREG.md` plus the protocol section into a 6+1 pp IEEE report.
   - Use the pilot only as feasibility and power justification.
   - The confirmatory hypotheses need outcome-neutral checks, such as positive controls (the seeded drifts must be detected) and v1 false-positive checks.
   - In-Principle Acceptance commits EMSE to the eventual confirmatory paper whatever the result. That is the strongest structure for this project.
   - SANER's scope (software evolution) fits better than MSR's, which requires repository data.
2. **ICSE 2027 AGENT workshop, full paper, 8 pp incl. refs, 13 Nov 2026.**
   - Single-anonymous review, so the public arXiv version and repo are no problem.
   - The topics match runtime monitoring, tool use and protocols almost word for word.
   - AGENTVERIFY (double-blind, same date) is the equal alternative. Do not submit to both.
3. **FSE 2027 Tool Demonstrations (26 Jan 2027, 4+1 pp + screencast).**
   - Archival ACM venue, with a single-blind review that suits a tool paper.
   - Content conflict: to keep this option, send the workshop paper to **LLM4Code's non-archival option** (13 Nov) rather than AGENT. Alternatively, accept that an archival AGENT paper probably rules out the FSE demo.
   - If the paper can be anonymized and reformatted by 31 Oct, PAgE @ POPL 2027 (non-archival) is a free extra venue for feedback.

### For the paper after the confirmatory study
1. **EMSE Stage 2**, through whichever RR track gives In-Principle Acceptance (MSR: submit by 30 Sep 2027; SANER: ~Dec 2027, TBC).
2. **A full research track at a top SE conference.**
   - ISSTA 2027 (paper 11 Jan 2027) only if the study is finished in time, which is unlikely.
   - Otherwise ASE 2027 or ICSE 2028 / FSE 2028. Their 2027–28 CFPs are **not yet published (UNVERIFIED)**, so re-check in Q1 2027.
3. **TMLR (rolling).** This is the right ML-facing venue for the agent-outcome results (pass^k before and after repair). Its criterion of claims backed by evidence rewards a pre-registered confirmatory design, and there is no deadline pressure.

### Cross-cutting
- **AI disclosure:**
  - Every IEEE venue (ICSE workshops, SANER, MSR, ICST) requires the AI system *and the affected sections* to be named in the acknowledgments.
  - At double-blind venues, keep the statement but anonymize it.
  - FSE requires research uses of AI to be described in the methods section.
  - Use one disclosure paragraph that meets the strictest rule (IEEE plus FSE) everywhere.
- **Before any submission:** verify every reference exists. A hallucinated reference brings a desk rejection at FSE and is the named trigger for the arXiv ban.
- **arXiv posting** is allowed by every venue above, but do not say on arXiv where the paper was submitted. For MSR double-anonymous tracks, use a different title for the preprint.
